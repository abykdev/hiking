No signal? Use satellite SOS

Many phones now (iPhone 14+, some newer Android) have built-in satellite emergency messaging for when there's no cell coverage — common on remote Armenian trails. Trailkin can't provide this itself; it's a feature of your phone.

How to check: Settings → Emergency SOS on iPhone, or your phone maker's safety app on Android.
