Good to know
What shoes do I need?

Closed-toe hiking shoes or boots with ankle support and grippy soles — trail runners work for easy/moderate trails, but avoid regular sneakers on rocky terrain.

How much water should I bring?

A rough rule of thumb: about 0.5 litres per hour of hiking, more in hot weather or on steep climbs.

What does elevation gain mean?

The total climbing you'll do over the whole hike, even if the trail goes up and down repeatedly — not just the difference between start and end altitude.

How hard is a 10 km hike?

Depends on elevation gain and terrain, not just distance — a flat 10 km is easy, 10 km with 800m+ of climbing is a serious hike.
